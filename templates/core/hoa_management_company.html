Who this is for

Self-managed HOAs and condos

Your board runs the community well, but the treasurer is doing the books at night and nobody wants the job next year. We take the accounting, the billing and the bank work; the board keeps everything else.

Boards leaving a full-service manager

You are paying full-service rates for a manager who mostly forwards emails. Keep your own vendors, keep the savings, and hand the part that needs an accountant to an accountant.

Boards that want clean, audit-ready books

An auditor, an escrow company or an owner has asked for records, and the answer took weeks. Statements that reconcile to the bank every month mean the next request takes a day.

How it works

  1. Ask for a quote. Tell us the number of units, where the association is, and what the board wants handled. You get a flat monthly fee and a clear scope, not a sales call.
  2. Send us the records. Governing documents, current budget, latest statements and bank statements, owner roster with balances, vendor list.
  3. We set up the books. Chart of accounts, owner accounts and balances, vendors, bank feeds. Owners get portal access for statements and online payments.
  4. The monthly rhythm starts. Assessments go out, contracted and recurring bills are paid on schedule, other invoices wait for board approval, accounts are reconciled, and the financial package lands in the board's inbox before the meeting.

Run by someone who has audited the books, not just kept them

Dynamite Management is led by Doug McLain, a former CPA who has audited and prepared tax returns for homeowners and condominium associations since 2011. Auditing other managers' books for years is a good way to learn what goes wrong in association accounting, and how to keep it from going wrong in yours.

The company's rule is simple: boards decide, we do the books. Bills the board has already committed to, such as vendor contracts, insurance installments and utilities on autopay, are paid on schedule; any other invoice waits for a board member's approval in the system before it goes out. Every account is reconciled to the bank statement every month, and the association's accounts stay in the association's name. If a board member wants to see any transaction, it is a login away, not a records request.

We are a small firm on purpose. You will deal with the people who actually do the work, and your monthly package will look the same in month twenty-four as it did in month one.
