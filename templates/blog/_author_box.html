About the author

Doug McLain

Doug McLain is a partner in Dynamite Management, LLC and the founder of HOA Fiscal. A former CPA, he has audited association financial statements since 2011, has provided audit, management and tax services to homeowner and condominium associations for more than 20 years, and is a co-author of Trade HOA Stress for Success, a board of directors' guide to running an association. General information, not legal or tax advice.
